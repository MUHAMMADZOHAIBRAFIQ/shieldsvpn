Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026. Applies to the ShieldsVPN apps for Android and Windows, the browser extension, and the ShieldsVPN website and account portal.

ShieldsVPN is developed and operated by Dua Software Studio, Pakistan ("we", "us"). Contact: muhammadzohaibrafiq2@gmail.com.

What we never collect

We do not log the websites or apps you use, the content of your traffic, or your DNS queries. The VPN server forwards your encrypted traffic and keeps no record of where it goes. The app does not read, inspect or sell the traffic that passes through Android's VPN service.

  • Ask for a copy of your data, or correction or deletion of it: muhammadzohaibrafiq2@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.
  • Change ad consent in the app's ad consent form, or reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Security

Connections use post-quantum cryptography (ML-KEM and ML-DSA, with an SLH-DSA root of trust, together with classical X25519), and the account portal uses HTTPS. On your phone the device key is protected by the Android Keystore; on Windows by DPAPI. No system is perfectly secure; report problems to muhammadzohaibrafiq2@gmail.com.

Children

ShieldsVPN is not directed at children and is meant for adults (18+).
